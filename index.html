A family stand, open after dark

Tamales Brenda's is run by Brenda and her son. Every tamal is made by hand, with light, scratch made masa and a generous filling, and the stand only opens on weekend nights.

Reviewers say a stop here feels like dinner at your aunt's house. Takeout from the window, or delivery through Grubhub.
