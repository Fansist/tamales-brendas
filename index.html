The stand

Feels like your aunt’s house

Tamales Brenda's is a family run tamale stand on Imperial Ave, open only on weekend nights. Brenda and her son make every tamale by hand with light, scratch made masa and generous fillings, alongside elotes, bacon wrapped hot dogs, and hot champurrado. Reviewers say a stop here feels like dinner at your aunt's house.

  • Scratch made masa, folded and steamed to order
  • A perfect 100/100 county health score
  • A regular in Yelp's best tamales roundups
